1.Arjit Singh

He gained prominence after participating in the reality show Fame Gurukul in 2005 and later became an assistant to music director Pritam. 2.Kishore Kumar

Kishore Kumar (born Abhas Kumar Ganguly on August 4, 1929, and passed away on October 13, 1987) was a highly versatile Indian playback singer, actor, composer, director, producer, and screenwriter. He is widely regarded as one of the most popular and influential singers in Hindi cinema.

4.Shreya Ghoshal

Shreya Ghoshal is an Indian playback singer known for her versatile voice and contributions to Indian cinema. Born on March 12, 1984, in Murshidabad, West Bengal, she began learning music at a young age and later gained prominence after winning the "Sa Re Ga Ma Pa" singing competition. She made her Bollywood debut with the film "Devdas" (2002) and has since sung in numerous Indian languages, earning multiple accolades, including five National Film Awards and seven Filmfare Awards

5.Neha Kakkar

Neha Kakkar is an Indian playback singer known for her work in Bollywood films. She gained prominence after participating in the second season of the reality show Indian Idol. She has since become a popular figure in the music industry, delivering numerous hit songs.
